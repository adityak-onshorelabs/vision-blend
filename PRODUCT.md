# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two visitors with equal weight. Founders and brand owners who found Vision Blend on Instagram or by referral, checking on a laptop or phone whether the studio has taste before they book a call. Marketing leads shortlisting agencies, who want to see real work and understand what the studio does. Both need to believe "these people make things that look considered" before they get in touch. The action is sending a brief through the contact form or messaging on Instagram.

## Product Purpose

Vision Blend is a marketing studio (since 2023, a team of six) working across branding and identity, film and production, and content and social. The site turns the credibility the studio builds on Instagram (Production Files, A Week at Vision Blend, its stated beliefs) into qualified briefs. Success: briefs received through the form, and Instagram DMs that mention the site.

## Brand Personality

Tasteful, warm, considered. The voice is the studio's own Instagram captions: short, plain, opinionated ("Good creative doesn't ask for attention. It earns it."). Emotional goal: "these people know what good looks like, and they'll make us look like ourselves."

## Anti-references

- AI-generated agency sites: glow gradients, glass cards, purple-to-blue heroes, "elevate your brand" copy.
- Identical icon card grids and big stat counters.
- Decorative props and skeuomorphism (paper clips, tilted cards, folder tabs, cream paper with a serif display). A first concept in that direction was rejected as reading like a wedding cake shop.
- Rounded pills and soft shadows: the client asked for sharp corners.

## Design Principles

1. Restraint is the brand. Detail comes from typography, spacing and one small motif (the full stop), not from props.
2. Their own words and work first. Real projects, captions and reels from Instagram, labelled as such; anything else is marked as a slot or TBC.
3. Every section is clearly separated: alternating bands with a numbered index row.
4. Interaction is quiet: hover and focus states confirm, they don't perform.
5. Every page is one step from "Start a project".

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA (all text tokens verified, lowest 4.51:1). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (all motion degrades to static). Touch targets at least 44px. Every interactive component keyboard operable with visible focus. Logical CSS properties so a second language can ship without rework. Plain English.
